The floor creaks under Amy's feet as she wipes the polished table with a cloth soaked in ale-scented oil until its surface gleams.
A green-skinned goblin leans over the counter clutching a chipped mug while Amy refills it by pouring liquid from a barrel beside the hearth.
The beautiful female elf steps into the tavern, silver hair streaming down her shoulders, takes a seat at the back table, and Amy pours a steaming cup into a crystal chalice for her, gently sets it on the wood with a nod of thanks.
From the doorway, a dragon-shaped creature slides onto a high bar stool, its obsidian scales glittering as its wings unfurl to reveal amber eyes that scan the room, while Amy greets it with a warm smile and lifts a crystal cup from her shelf, pouring a special brew into it before handing it over.
The dragon licks its lips then takes a slow sip letting liquid swirl across its tongue as Amy watches it exhale contentedly, wiping the bar with a fresh cloth to smooth out any drips of ale that have fallen onto the wood.
Amy turns off the lanterns at each table and pulls a wooden latch on the back door, steps outside to close it, locking the tavern for the night as the moon rises over the medieval town.
