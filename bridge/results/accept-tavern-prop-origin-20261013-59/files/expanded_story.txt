The floor creaks as Amy steps onto it, then she lifts a rough‑spun cloth soaked in ale‑scented oil and wipes down the polished tabletop with sweeping strokes. She wears a tunic of coarse wool and a leather apron that show scuffs from her medieval trade.

A goblin with green skin leans over the counter; its tiny hands clutch a chipped mug. Amy reaches for a barrel beside the hearth, pours ale into the goblin’s mug, and sets it on the counter while the creature nods gratefully.

An elf with silver hair that streams down her shoulders steps in through the door and takes a seat at the back table. Amy moves to the kitchen counter, scoops steaming liquid from a pot, pours it into a crystal chalice for the elf, then places the chalice gently on the wooden surface of the back table while offering a nod of thanks.

From the doorway, a dragon‑shaped creature slides onto a high bar stool; its scales glitter like polished obsidian. The dragon unfurls wings that reveal amber eyes scanning the room. Amy greets it with a warm smile and lifts a crystal cup from her shelf, pours a special brew into it, then hands the cup to the dragon.

The dragon licks its lips, takes a slow sip, letting the liquid swirl across its tongue. Amy watches as the creature exhales contentedly; she wipes the bar with a fresh cloth, smoothing out any drips of ale that have fallen onto the wood.

Finally, Amy turns off each lantern at every table and pulls a wooden latch on the back door. She steps outside, closes the latch, locks the tavern for the night as the moon rises over the medieval town.
