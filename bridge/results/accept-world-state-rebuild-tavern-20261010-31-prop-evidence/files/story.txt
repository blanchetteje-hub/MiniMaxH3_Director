The wooden floor creaks as Amy wipes a polished table with a cloth soaked in ale-scented oil. She wears a rough-spun tunic and leather apron that show the scuffs of her medieval trade. A goblin with green skin leans over the counter, its tiny hands clutching a chipped mug; Amy refills it from a barrel beside the hearth.

A beautiful female elf steps in, silver hair streaming down its shoulders, and takes a seat at the back table. Amy pours a steaming cup into a crystal chalice for the elf, setting it gently on the wood with a nod of thanks.

From the doorway, a dragon-shaped creature slides onto a high bar stool, scales glittering like polished obsidian. Its wings unfurl to reveal a pair of amber eyes that scan the room. Amy greets the newcomer with a warm smile and lifts a crystal cup from her shelf, pouring a special brew into it before handing it over.

The dragon licks its lips, then takes a slow sip, letting the liquid swirl across its tongue. Amy watches as the creature exhales contentedly; she wipes the bar with a fresh cloth, smoothing out any drips of ale that have fallen onto the wood.

Finally, Amy turns off the lanterns at each table and pulls a wooden latch on the back door. She steps outside to close it, locking the tavern for the night as the moon rises over the medieval town.
