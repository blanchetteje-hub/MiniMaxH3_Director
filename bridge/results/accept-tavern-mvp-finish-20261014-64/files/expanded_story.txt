The wooden floor creaks when Amy wipes a polished table with a cloth soaked in ale‑scented oil.  
She wears a rough‑spun tunic and a leather apron that show the scuffs of her medieval trade.  

A goblin with green skin leans over the counter, its tiny hands clutching a chipped mug; Amy refills it from a barrel beside the hearth by lifting the mug and pouring ale into it.  

A beautiful female elf steps in, silver hair streaming down its shoulders, takes a seat at the back table; Amy pours a steaming cup into a crystal chalice for the elf, sets the chalice gently on the wood with a nod of thanks.  

From the doorway a dragon‑shaped creature slides onto a high bar stool; scales glitter like polished obsidian and wings unfurl to reveal amber eyes that scan the room.  
Amy greets the newcomer with a warm smile and lifts a crystal cup from her shelf, pours a special brew into it, then hands it over.  

The dragon licks its lips, takes a slow sip, letting liquid swirl across its tongue; Amy watches as the creature exhales contentedly. She wipes the bar with a fresh cloth, smoothing out any drips of ale that have fallen onto the wood.  

Finally Amy turns off lanterns at each table and pulls a wooden latch on the back door.  
She steps outside to close it, locking the tavern for the night as the moon rises over the medieval town.
