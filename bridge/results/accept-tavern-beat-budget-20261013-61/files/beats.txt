# Phase 1
1. The wooden floor creaks as Amy wipes a polished table with a cloth soaked in ale‑scented oil, her rough‑spun tunic and leather apron scuffed from medieval trade.
2. A goblin with green skin leans over the counter clutching a chipped mug; Amy refills it from a barrel beside the hearth.
3. An elf with silver hair steps in, takes a seat at the back table, and Amy pours a steaming cup into a crystal chalice for the elf, setting the chalice gently on the wood while nodding thanks.
4. From the doorway, a dragon‑shaped creature slides onto a high bar stool, scales glittering like polished obsidian, wings unfurl to reveal amber eyes that scan the room; Amy greets it with a warm smile and lifts a crystal cup from her shelf, pouring a special brew into it before handing it to the dragon.
5. The dragon licks its lips then takes a slow sip, letting liquid swirl across its tongue while Amy watches as it exhales contentedly; she wipes the bar with a fresh cloth smoothing any drips of ale that have fallen onto the wood.
6. Finally, Amy turns off the lanterns at each table and pulls a wooden latch on the back door, steps outside to close it, locking the tavern for the night as the moon rises over the medieval town.
