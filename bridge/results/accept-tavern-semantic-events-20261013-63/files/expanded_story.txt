The floor creaks as Amy wipes the polished table with a cloth soaked in ale‑scented oil. She wears a rough‑spun tunic and a leather apron, both scuffed from her medieval trade. A goblin with green skin leans over the counter; its tiny hands clutch a chipped mug. Amy refills that mug from a barrel beside the hearth.

A beautiful female elf steps in, silver hair streaming down her shoulders as she takes a seat at the back table. Amy pours a steaming cup into a crystal chalice for the elf and sets it gently on the wood, nodding with thanks.

From the doorway a dragon‑shaped creature slides onto a high bar stool; its scales glitter like polished obsidian. Its wings unfurl to reveal amber eyes that scan the room. Amy greets the newcomer with a warm smile, lifts a crystal cup from her shelf, pours a special brew into it, and hands it over.

The dragon licks its lips then takes a slow sip, letting the liquid swirl across its tongue. Amy watches as the creature exhales contentedly; she wipes the bar with a fresh cloth, smoothing out any drips of ale that have fallen onto the wood.

Finally Amy turns off the lanterns at each table and pulls a wooden latch on the back door. She steps outside to close it, locking the tavern for the night as the moon rises over the medieval town.
