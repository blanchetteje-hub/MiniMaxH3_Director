Amy stands behind a rough?hewn wooden bar in a stone tavern that smells of woodsmoke and stew. A large dragon with scales like polished bronze steps onto the far end of the bar, claws tapping the floor as it settles on a wide stool. Amy lifts a heavy oak barrel from its rack, opens the iron lid, and pours amber ale into a crystal goblet, letting the liquid spill over the rim onto the counter. A unicorn with a silver mane saunters to the middle of the tavern, steps onto a low chair, and drinks from a clear crystal cup that Amy places on the table in front of it. Amy wipes down the bar with a damp cloth, smoothing the wood until it gleams under the flickering torches. The room fills with laughter as more magical humanoid creatures—elves, goblins, centaurs, and fairies—enter, sit at tables, and chat over mugs of ale.
